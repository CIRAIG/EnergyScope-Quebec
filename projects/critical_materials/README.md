# Critical materials tracking for EnergyScope Pathway

## Context

Endogenous integration of material constraints and dynamic recycling loops in EnergyScope-Québec.

The material intensities of the technologies (mostly from Bieuville, Majeau-Bettez and de Bortoli, 2025, see `excel_files/README.md`) are linked to the installed capacities of the pathway model, so that the material demand of the transition is a result of the optimisation and can be limited. The recycling loops bring the material of decommissioned technologies back against the demand of the following years.

## Usage

The recommended way to run the model with materials is `run_pathway(..., materials=True)` in `shared/utils.py`, which calls `run_pathway_materials()` in this folder. Run it from `projects/critical_materials/`. See `tutorial_pathway_materials.ipynb` for a full walkthrough: basic runs, reading the material results, the dashboard, the recycling approaches, material limits, and the PV and wind sub-technologies. Without `materials=True`, `run_pathway()` behaves as on `main`.

The materials options are `materials_recycling`, `materials_recycling_cost`, `force_max_recycling`, `force_immediate_recycled_use`, `materials_limit`, `materials_recycling_process`, `mip_gap`, `build_dashboard` and `open_dashboard`. They are documented in the docstring of `run_pathway_materials()`. The usual `run_pathway()` options (`N_year_opti`, `gwp_budget`, `extra_files`, `save_pkl`, ...) still apply. For example:

    from shared.utils import run_pathway
    results = run_pathway('my_run', materials=True, materials_recycling=True,
                          materials_recycling_cost=False, force_max_recycling=True)

## Results

The main axes covered are:
- `Material_content_year`: the annual material demand of each EnergyScope technology
- `Material_content_cumulative`: the cumulated annual material demand of each EnergyScope technology
- `Recycled_material`: the quantity of material recycled each year

The other result keys are listed in the project status below.

## Project status
In progress (14-10-2026)

This folder adds an endogenous tracking of 41 critical materials to the pathway model. `Constraints.mod` is read after `QC_es_main.mod` and `PES_main.mod`, whose variables it uses (`F_new`, `F_decom`, `F_old`, `C_tot_capex`, `C_tot_opex`). The gross demand of a year is the material intensity times the new capacity of the phase (`Material_content_year`). The material of decommissioned technologies (`Decommissioned_material`) is recycled up to `recycling_rate` (`Recycled_material`), and the rest is disposed (`Disposed_material`). The recycled material used against the gross demand of the year is `Used_recycled_material`, and what is not used accumulates in `Material_stock`. The net demand (gross demand minus used recycled material) is bounded each year by `limit_material_year`, and its cumulative sum by `limit_material`.

`C_material` (recycling cost, disposal cost, avoided primary material cost) is added to the transition cost. `Constraints.mod` replaces the `total_cost_transition` constraint of `PES_main.mod` with `total_cost_transition_materials`, so that `PES_main.mod` stays identical to `main`. Annual values are given at the last year of each phase, and cumulative values multiply them by 5.

With `force_immediate_recycled_use=True`, a big-M constraint with one binary per year and material imposes that, every year, either the stock is empty or the demand is covered, so recycled material is used before being banked. `stocking_price`, an artificial cost on `Material_stock`, is set by `run_pathway_materials()`: 1 with `materials_limit=True` and `force_immediate_recycled_use=False`, 1000 otherwise. These binaries can make the MIP much slower, from a few minutes to more than a quarter of an hour depending on the scenario. `mip_gap` only helps once a first integer solution is found. With `force_immediate_recycled_use=False` the binaries have no effect, and the run takes a few minutes.

With `materials=True`, PV and wind are modelled through 20 sub-technologies, because their materials differ: 4 types of PV (`C_SI`, `CDTE`, `CIGS`, `A_SIGE`) and 4 types of wind turbine (`DD_EESG`, `GB_DFIG_SCIG`, `DD_PMSG`, `GB_PMSG`), in 5 families (`PV_ROOF`, `PV_GROUND`, `WIND_ONSHORE`, `NEW_WIND_ONSHORE`, `WIND_OFFSHORE`). The aggregated technologies of `main` stay in the model with `f_min = f_max = 0`, so only their sub-technologies produce. `subtechs.py` merges the sub-technologies' rows with the pathway tables (`years_active`, `remaining_years`, `AGE`) at run time, and no shared file is modified for this. `Subtechs_market_share.mod` can fix the share between the sub-technologies of a family; without it, the optimiser chooses the mix freely.

None of the `.dat` files of materials is written by hand, except `Material_impacts.dat`. They are generated from `excel_files/` by the `run_build_*.py` scripts (see `excel_files/README.md`), so edit the workbook and rerun the script instead of editing a `.dat`. The recycling process approach (`materials_recycling_process`) is a prototype: it covers PV, electric vehicles (battery, motor, chassis) and the wind turbine motor. The infrastructure and blades of wind turbines, and the chassis of internal combustion vehicles, are not modelled yet.

Results are written in `out/<run>/`: `_Results.pkl`, `_Materials_Results.pkl`, `csv/`, `graphs/` (the dashboard) and `log.txt`, and `out/index.html` is a selector between all the scenarios. `results` holds the usual pathway DataFrames plus the material flows (`Material_content_year`, `Decommissioned_material`, `Recycled_material`, `Disposed_material`, `Used_recycled_material`, `Net_demand`), `Material_stock`, `Recycling_benefit`, their cumulative versions, `limit_material_year` and the scalars `C_material` and `C_material_recycling_tech`. `impacts.py` computes the avoided environmental impact as the used recycled material times the difference between the primary and secondary impact, shown in the "Avoided impact" page of the dashboard. `Material_impacts.dat` only contains aluminium so far, and a material that is missing has no avoided impact.

Outside this folder, `shared/utils.py` only dispatches `run_pathway(materials=True)` to `run_pathway_materials()`. `shared/data/QC_data.dat` no longer lists `DEC_HP_ELEC_WINTER` and `DEC_HP_ELEC_PEAK`, which made a plain `run_pathway()` fail because they are missing from the pathway tables. `projects/pathway/src/plot_results.py` only gets `auto_open` and a generic reading of named groups. The materials pages and the fixed colours are registered at run time by `Plot_functions.load_plot_results()`.
